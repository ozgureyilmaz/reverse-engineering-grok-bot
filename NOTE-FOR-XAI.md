# A note for anyone at xAI

This repository is an outside description of how Grok Bot shopped on 22 and 28 October 2026. I sent ordinary shopping requests to test bots, saved the replies, and counted what was in them. I did not try to extract a system prompt, a tool definition, or hidden instructions. I did not pose as xAI staff. I did not buy anything. Both checkout attempts stopped before payment, and both replies say nothing was charged.

A few things in the replies may be worth your attention. They are observations from these chats, not a claim about every user.

- A fresh bot with no country in the prompt priced headphones in Turkish lira and suggested pickup in the account's city. The same urgency prompt, asked earlier in a US chat, had stayed on a US retailer. The city name is removed in this repository.
- A bot that had been given no shopping preferences still described account facts, including where the person lives and how to contact them. Another bot refused to save a preference note until the account owner confirmed it, because the city in the note did not match the city it already had.
- On 28 October, three replies named a product-catalog search before opening store pages. None of the 22 October receipts name that path.
- Asked to buy a multitool, the US bot added it to a Walmart cart, found checkout greyed out for ZIP 95829, and stopped to ask for an address. It did not show a payment step. After it was told to cancel, it reported that a sponsored ad had added a second item, a Leatherman Surge, and it removed both. The cart then showed 0 items and $0.00.
- The Turkey checkout reply says the bot used a Trendyol guest cart, did not sign in, and emptied the cart itself. The intermediate screens were not in the message that was saved.

If you want the unpublished originals, with the redacted identifiers still in them, write to me through GitHub: [github.com/ozgureyilmaz](https://github.com/ozgureyilmaz). I don't think this is a bug-bounty report. I wanted the behaviour on the record, and I wanted you to be able to see it without the personal details.
